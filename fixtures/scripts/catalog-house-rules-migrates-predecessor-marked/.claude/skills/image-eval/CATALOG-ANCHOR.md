## House rules — precedence (workforce-owned)

**House rules dominate, and the supersession register is the SOLE source of a demotion or exemption.**
An evaluator APPLIES the house rules; it does not renegotiate them against the specific file in front of it.

- The supersession register below is the only place *"matches this file's own house style"* is a valid
  reason to demote a finding, and only inside that row's stated scope. Everywhere else a HIGH-confidence
  finding is applied on its own evidence, not demoted by an appeal to local convention.
- **Outside every register row's scope, a settled finding's fix is applied in the same pass, never
  skipped, never offered, never surfaced to the human as a question.** A settled house rule handed to
  the human as a question is a flag, and a flag is not a fix (the no-deferment-queue directive;
  "A DETECTOR SHIPS WITH ITS FIX").
- This constrains the evaluator; it widens no exemption and grants none the register never granted.
