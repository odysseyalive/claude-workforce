Work list: a continuation lands on the issue it names, open or closed

Measured 2026-10-29 in mutze-studio 29c3e592 on 1.82.1: i8 only added
context to i7, which was already closed, and wrote the one item the
checklist line asks for, "issue": "i7", "status": "answered". The
ledger shows i8 closed as an issue of its own holding that item, its
"issue" key dropped, and i7 with its original four items. absorb_items
built its merge targets from open_issues alone, so a continuation of a
closed issue was never moved.

Changes:
- wf-task-tag absorb_items takes every issue in the ledger as a target
  except its own and a folded one ("merged", which holds nothing). Items
  merge by wording as before.
- A moved item that is not finished (item_done false) reopens a closed
  target, since new work arrived on it. Finished items leave it closed.
- The own issue folds exactly as before when every item moved, and never
  over a running agent. absorb_carried still ignores closed issues.
- The checklist sentence now says an item continues "an earlier issue,
  open or closed", and the context-only sentence says "an earlier
  issue". Each is still one sentence.

A reopened issue is state "open" with a written open item, so heal_empty
does not take it as stale, refresh_index lists it with items_open 1,
and prune_dead_rows keeps it for a dead session (over_when_dead judges
it kickback).

seed a closed i1, write i2's item with "issue": "i1", and absorb it at

i2 and folds nothing. issues-context-only-message-folds-into-its-issue
pins the reworded sentence and passes. script-conformance 673/673,
