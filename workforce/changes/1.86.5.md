Issues record when they open and close, so a replay carries what a past Stop carried

wf-task-tag stamps opened_at on a new issue, closed_at when it closes
(dropped on reopen), and a done_at map keyed by a digest of each item's
words when an item finishes. bin/replay-seating uses them: a Stop carries
an issue opened before its turn and not closed before it, with items
done later replayed as open. A legacy issue closed with no time says
"closed, time unknown" instead of reading as unseated.

Found when mutze 30e77777 Stop 2007 replayed as unseated after its issue
i1 closed, though it had carried i1 live.
