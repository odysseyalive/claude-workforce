A turn that asks for nothing is still tried on the session's open work

When a turn has no docket from its own words, a peer's message, the ask
its injected opener serves, or a message delivered mid-turn, it inherits
the dockets of the open issues its asks checklist carries. A carried hit
only seats a juror: it never blocks on a channel count, never seats the
incident jurors, survives a declared hold, and the jurors are told never
to vote no because that work is unfinished. Peer-opened issues are marked
so their words never read as the user's.

Measured: on mutze 30e77777 the two missed "still on the pod" Stops (2007,
2203) and 2751/2785 now seat touch; over 1,323 mutze Stops it adds 19.6 to
34.5 seats per 100 Stops; 9 of 10 sampled new seats tried a real claim.

User: "This just needs to be smart enough to manage everything, you know,
within these senses."
