A score is not a tally of activity, and nobody grades their own paper (v1.43.0)

TWO USER DIRECTIVES, captured verbatim, stated minutes apart and recorded together
because they are one rule seen from both ends:

  "Evaluations are always done by the independent agent"

  "Just running tools doesn't mean that you're doing a good job. It's the evidence
   from the tool run comparative to the results from the ask ... you have to measure
   results, not measure what you're doing and it has to be aligned with the current
   request."

WHAT PROMPTED THEM, confirmed from the code before either was stated. `wf-widen`
scored a turn 100/100 because it made two Bash calls. Asked what backed that number,
the enumeration is unambiguous: the `2/1/0 -> 100/50/0` thresholds appear EXACTLY ONCE
in the repository, at `wf-widen:339`, with no comment and no measurement anywhere. The
counts feeding them are real transcript events; the scale on top of them was invented.
`100/100` reads as "this work was complete" and means "two shell commands ran" — a
real count wearing a fake scale, which is `enforcement.md`'s own overclaiming defect
one level up.

AND IT GRADED ITSELF. `wf-widen` is a deterministic hook running in-session at `Stop`.
The one independent component, `wf-widen-agent`, is gated on a flag that is only
written when the score BLOCKS — so a passing turn is audited by nothing but the session
that produced it, and a high score SUPPRESSES the only outside reader. The file's own
docstring argues for independence at length and the scorer was built in-session anyway.

THE TWO CLAUSES COMPOSE RATHER THAN STACK. You cannot measure results with a counter,
because comparing evidence against a request means reading both — so "measure results"
FORCES "independent agent". It also unlocks the sixth sense: `intuition` shipped
permanently unscored because judging one's own prior from inside is the impaired
faculty, and that objection dissolves the moment the judge is somebody else.

WHAT SURVIVES AND WHAT DOES NOT, said plainly so measured work is not thrown out with
the invented part. The claim TRIGGERS are evidence-based and stand: 0.5% and 1.5% over
1,495 real turns, 9.4% and 0.9% over 106. Those measure WHEN TO LOOK and they held. It
is the SCORING that was fabricated. Dispatch survives the coming redesign; the grade
does not.
