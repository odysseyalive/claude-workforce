Add tunnel vision section to Why This Exists

The most capable models are the most prone to locking onto a reading
and not questioning it. Research backs this up: choice-supportive bias
(Kumaran et al., Nature Machine Intelligence 2026), sycophancy
(Sharma et al., Anthropic), and confirmation bias (Jhaveri et al.).
This project measured it too: 9 of 10 escapes from a wrong reading
came from outside the turn, zero from noticing.

The widening mechanism breaks the tunnel by spawning an independent
reader that checks whether the evidence matches the conclusion.
