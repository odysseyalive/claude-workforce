Say the state, not the journey (v1.26.0)

Two user directives, seven messages, captured verbatim in SKILL.md.

FINISH, DO NOT HEDGE. A question whose options are all "do less" is a
deferment queue with a nicer interface. Measured: a run brought four of
five handbooks under the ceiling, could not reach the fifth by the moves
it was making, and asked the user to pick between four approaches — the
first being "leave it, with the reason written down". Their answer:
"None of these options sound like you are wanting to fix the issue."

SAY THE STATE, NOT THE JOURNEY. The same disease on the report instead
of the question. A run fixed eight assigned items and eleven it found on
the way, then narrated each as a discovery with its resolution somewhere
else in the paragraph. Every sentence was true. "are you going to fix
it, or are you just telling me about the problem?" — then "It feels like
deception ... it leaves me wondering whether you are compentant."
Accuracy is not the standard; the standard is that the reader finishes
the reply knowing the state. Three clauses: a resolved thing is never
reported as unresolved, work is never handed over that you could do, and
a finding is never retroactively downgraded to a non-finding.

handbook template and wf-conform carry it: the output style and the Stop
guard reach the main conversation only, because a subagent runs its own
system prompt. Without those two carriers every agent's report stayed
ungoverned, one tier below anyone who could see it.
