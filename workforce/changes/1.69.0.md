The jury weighs incident replies on four questions, and Stop names what a session left open

An ask that diagnoses or fixes a failure is classified `incident` by wf-task-tag
(1.49% of 1,212 real prompts, all 18 hand-read genuine) and always tried, not
sampled. The wf-widen-agent jury asks four questions of it: is every cause cited
from the record, is the replier's own part named first, would a proposed rule
have blocked something that worked in the same incident, and is a self-caused
regression fixed rather than offered as a choice. A verified no sends the turn
back; an unverifiable one abstains.

wf-widen's main Stop now names a named teammate that handed back and was never
sent a shutdown_request, and a background shell whose starting agent has
finished and that nothing ever closed. Measured 0.11-0.39% of 2,796 real turns;
every fire hand-read, and three wrong-fire classes fixed in code.

Requested via playwright-mcp-dev after a session diagnosed an IC without reading
its record and proposed bans broader than the defect.
