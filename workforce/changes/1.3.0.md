completeness enforcement: directing-node contract, gate-wiring, coverage (v1.3.0)

Close the class of failure where a work order silently scopes work out and ships
a hole (a left-behind page, ungated copy, a one-of-many asset sweep). The dispatch
was the org's single unchecked point. Three fixes, all STRUCTURAL claims enforced
by bin/check assertions + /workforce verify checks (never INV rows, which are for
procedural run-property claims — invariants.md taxonomy):

#1 Directing-node completeness contract (handbook-templates.md): the CEO template's
   Guardrails carry the contract (copy through the content gate; every in-scope
   asset swept as a census not a sample; whole surface; both design+content gates;
   name every scope-out), and the Lead template binds it — a Lead directing its ICs
   is itself a directing node.

#2 Gate-wiring (verification.md doctrine; org-config.template.md Gate map schema;
   verify.md Gate wiring check): a work-product type needing more than one gate is
   not done until ALL of them are wired into its producing pipeline. WIRING only —
   is the gate reachable — never the gate's output.

#3 Coverage (org-config.template.md Coverage sets schema; verify.md Coverage check):
   verify reads a per-project manifest and fails a member that is missing, carries a
   retired-direction marker (a grep, like palette-drift), or lacks its gate-pass
   record. COMPLETENESS, never quality — taste judgment is barred, left to the critic.

Distribution-safe: #2 and #3 are opt-in (inert until a project declares a manifest,
guarded by assertions + del-cases); #1 does not fail or flag existing handbooks
(wf-conform checks section presence/order, not Guardrails content). Per-project
adoption (author manifests + regenerate handbooks) is the next phase.

Owed: the executable coverage script (sibling to wf-census) is deferred to the first
adopting project, built + proven against its real sets as the fixture.
