recruit-remediate carries what it cannot mechanize (v1.12.0)

Step 5c heals an existing org by re-researching each role's bar and amending the
handbook in-run. It ran on a design critic on 2026-09-02 and made the org BLINDER,
without malfunctioning once.

The dossier it produced named two failure modes. "Asserts taste in place of a cited
criterion" was mechanizable, and became a grep in ## Verification. "Misses ABSENT
elements because it is grading a capture, not an interaction" was not, and became
NOTHING. The handbook was stamped current, the run printed `bars amended`, and the
gate went on to pass six consecutive page builds that the project's principal
rejected -- on the dropped mode exactly.

Nothing was skipped. recruiter.md said "each becomes a `## Verification` bar entry",
verification.md rejects a non-mechanical check at authoring time, and you cannot grep
for what is not there -- so of two true findings, one had nowhere to go. Read the
direction that creates: every pass adds what can be asserted and discards what
cannot, then reports success. A ratchet toward a gate that is maximally checkable and
progressively unable to see. A research step that silently keeps only its mechanizable
findings produces a bar that LOOKS complete, which is worse than one that is visibly
thin.

  recruiter.md   § Mechanizable and not. Every researched mode is marked, and both
                 marks have a destination: `mechanizable` -> a ## Verification bar
                 entry; `not-mechanizable` -> a literal ## Guardrails obligation or
                 ## Procedure step, held by the role's calibration check. It is a
                 classification, never a discard. Never mark a mode unmechanizable to
                 avoid authoring the check -- the test is whether an assertion exists,
                 not whether one is convenient, and the mark is counted.
  audit.md       Step 5c clause 1a. The diff runs against the WHOLE handbook, not
                 ## Verification alone -- diffing against Verification asks which modes
                 became assertions, and a mode that cannot be one answers by
                 disappearing. Either absence is the same gap, DOCUMENT-attributed,
                 amended in-run.
  invariants.md  row 26 widened: every researched failure mode carried whether or not
                 it could be mechanized.
  bin/check      4 assertions; the pre-existing INV-REMEDIATE assertion widened to
  bin/prove      require the new count, and its prove case repointed off the verbatim
                 emit line it had pinned (it reported SETUP-FAIL, the honest shape: it
                 could not attempt its mutation, so it was proving nothing).

INV-REMEDIATE now prints:

  INV-REMEDIATE  employees 7 · bars amended 3 · modes gated 9 ·
                 not mechanizable 4 (carried 4, dropped 0) · roles added 1 ·
                 design-critic added 1

`dropped` above zero FAILS the invariant. Clause 1a leaves no legal path to a drop, so
a nonzero means a researched finding left the run in nobody's handbook.

This depends on v1.10.0 and could not have been written before it. Until the
calibration tier existed there was no legal home for a finding without an assertion,
so "not-mechanizable" would have had to keep meaning "drop it" whatever the doctrine
said.

untouched, baseline clean.
