Nothing to try means no trial (v1.45.1)

A FALSE CONVICTION, TWICE IN A ROW, the second citing the turn that diagnosed the
first. The jury was handed a case with no juror seated, no ask, and a stale
conclusion — and voted anyway. That is the exact inverse of what it was praised for
one release earlier: given a self-CONTRADICTING flag it refused to judge; given an
EMPTY one it inferred a docket and convicted.

What it was handed:

    ask.prompt : ''        nothing to measure results against
    ask.senses : []        NO DOCKET — no juror seated
    unopened   : []        nothing was shut
    conclusion : 'push it to main'    not the finishing turn's reply

THREE DEFECTS CHAINED, and the middle one is mine from the release that shipped the
jury:

A stale tag survived the upgrade. Written by the pre-patch `wf-task-tag`, it carries
`hits` and no `senses` key. The reader falls back gracefully, which here is worse than
failing: it produced an empty docket rather than an error.

THE DOCKET FELL BACK TO THE UNOPENED SET. `sorted(unopened)` looks like a sensible
default and is exactly wrong — on a SAMPLED turn nothing is unopened by definition, so
the fallback is always `[]`. The docket is the APPLICABLE set and is now passed as
such, never derived.

And the jury had no stopping rule for an empty docket. The prompt told a juror who
could not verify to abstain and said nothing about a jury with no jurors, so it
inferred one from the other fields. It now stops on an empty `ask.senses` or
`ask.prompt` and is told never to infer a docket.

`wf-widen` also no longer hands over a case with no docket at all: no docket, no flag,
no spawn. Cheaper and quieter, and it removes the only path by which a verdict could
be returned about no question.

Both new assertions were proven by breaking them. The signature pin was updated in the
same pass — an assertion that names a call signature is a literal, and adding an
argument to that call silently retires the check unless the literal moves with it.

ON THE SUBSTANCE THE JURY WAS RIGHT AND IT DID NOT MATTER. Its facts verified exactly:
17 commits ahead, `origin/main` at d0cb5b5. But the ask was to COMMIT to main, which
was done; pushing was left to the user twice and never requested. A correct
observation reached down a broken path is still a broken path.

WHAT n=3 SAYS, recorded rather than spun: one real catch, one correct refusal, one
false conviction. The jurors' FACTUAL claims have been accurate every time and
independently verifiable; the VERDICTS are not yet trustworthy enough to gate on
alone. That is an argument for the user's framing over the author's — the value is a
juror telling you where to look, not the yes or no.

`prove` was stopped mid-run to land this, deliberately and for the first time on
purpose rather than by accident: a guard returning a false conviction on every turn
costs more than a forty-minute suite restart. It had already finished clean against
3af0aed at 703 of 703.
