A round that ends under its budget is an agent that came back, not one that was cut

The cap test read the CUMULATIVE turn count, which never returns to zero across a
SendMessage resume, so every stop after the first cut read as a cap stop forever.
Measured 2026-10-13 in session 83ab658c: a cap-10 filer cut in rounds 1 and 2 wrote
its last four files in round 3 and returned, and the stop filed `turn cap - filer
stopped at turn 3 of 10 (round 3)` and left the entry `handoff`, so the next main
Stop would have demanded a RESUME of an agent that had finished. The test is now the
round's own final threshold, which is the line wf-turn-budget schedules its notice
on, and a round that ends under it retires the handoff an earlier round left.

The turn number was one short for a second reason. The writer's last PostToolUse
fires inside the host's 100 ms transcript batch, so the row carrying the call it
fired for is never in the file it reads, and no later call exists after a stop to
pick it up: the counter read 23 turns and 23 writes against 24 tool-call rows and 24
Write calls. The stop now folds that tail in itself, through wf-turn-budget's own
fold_tail and final_pending rather than a second model of its own.
