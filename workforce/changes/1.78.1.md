ISSUES lines are status, title and the user's words; closed issues are not listed

User, 2026-10-14: "We don't need to say one item's done or three agents
returned or, you know, two agents processing or working. The title just needs
to go there. And it just needs to say processing or, you know, if it's on hold,
it's on hold." Then: "I don't think we need to have Closed either because that
list can grow fairly large and why have a bunch of lines that say Closed?"

Each line is now `<id>  <STATUS>  <title>  [<excerpt>]`; the counts and reasons
are gone (the session reads open items from its carried list), the header is
just ISSUES, and an issue that closed is not printed — the ledger keeps it.

Demonstrated on a throwaway project, real hooks: an issue with an agent working
prints `i1  PROCESSING  Agenda MA charts  [...]`; an unfinished one prints
KICKBACK with its title; one finished in its own turn is not listed.
