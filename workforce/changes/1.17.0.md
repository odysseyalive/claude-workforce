wf-stamp: contract-stamp had six readers and no writer (v1.17.0)

`contract-stamp` hashes a handbook's normalized `## Procedure` + `## Verification`.
A recomputation that differs means the employee's contract moved and its eval
baseline is stale - that is CONTRACT-DRIFT, and six shipped surfaces read the
field to detect it:

  org.md § Mode: index step 4     classifies CONTRACT-DRIFT from it
  verify.md § Org integrity       reports CONTRACT-DRIFT from it
  review.md step 1                opens on recomputing it
  checksums.md § What is stamped  lists it as a stamped target
  amend.md, handbook.md           each say "recompute the contract-stamp"
  org-chart-format.md             documents the field and its drift meaning

Nothing wrote it. handbook-templates.md ships the line as the literal placeholder
`sha256:<of ## Procedure + ## Verification, normalized>`, checksums is
executor-followed and only its directive-sidecar half was ever performed, and no
T-step in hire.md named a stamping action. So all six compared a recomputation
against a placeholder, and CONTRACT-DRIFT was unreachable on every org this
product has built.

MEASURED 2026-09-05 by /workforce verify on playwright-mcp: six live employees,
five carrying the literal string `(set at registration)` and engineering-lead
carrying no stamp line at all. That sixth case is why the producer does not just
substitute a placeholder - the template line is not reliably emitted, so a
placeholder-only writer would have missed one handbook in six and reported clean.

A consumer named and a producer assumed, for the fourth recorded time -
.directives.sha, platform-local.md, the settings write, now this.

  workforce/bin/wf-stamp   NEW. Display by default, --execute writes, --restamp
      overwrites a MISMATCH. Governed-only by the ORG-RECORD marker, the same
      discriminator wf-conform uses: an adopted agent was censused with zero bytes
      changed and stamping one writes workforce's field into a file it does not own.
      section() and normalize() are copied verbatim from wf-conform with a comment
      naming the source - checksums.md rule 1, two readers of one artifact share one
      grammar, and this distribution expresses that by copying rather than importing.
      Reads back and re-parses with its own reader before counting a write, per
      checksums.md rule 2.

MISMATCH IS FLAGGED AND NEVER SILENTLY REPAIRED (checksums.md § On mismatch). A
differing stamp is either a legitimate amendment that did not restamp or an edit
nobody authorized, and the hash cannot tell them apart; re-stamping erases the
only evidence that something changed. Default refuses and exits 1 even under
--execute. `--restamp` is the separate gesture and amend.md is the one caller that
makes it, because there the amendment IS the authorization. Writing a FIRST stamp
over a placeholder or an absent line is not a repair - there is no prior claim -
so that happens under plain --execute.

THE STAGED COPY IS WRITTEN TOO, and it is not an extra. hire.md T6 verifies the
registered file's sha against the staged handbook's, and wf-conform asserts the
same equality. Stamping one and not the other manufactures a divergence in the
record that proves a registration was verified. The digest depends only on
Procedure + Verification, identical in both copies, so both get the same line and
stay byte-equal.

Lifecycle: hire.md T1, and NOT a new T-step - the T-order is this project's most
safety-critical constant and nothing moved. Stamping produces the staged artifact.
It goes at T1 rather than after T5 because of T6: a stamp written after
registration changes the registered bytes and leaves T6's sha describing a file
that no longer exists, which is the exact divergence T6 exists to catch,
manufactured by the step meant to close a gap. The field lives in ORG-RECORD,
which is not part of either hashed section, so writing it cannot change the digest
it records - no second pass, no recursion.

Six fixtures, and the two negatives are the load-bearing ones:

  stamp-placeholder      display writes nothing; `after` proves the file is untouched
  stamp-execute          `after` proves the placeholder is GONE and a digest landed
  stamp-absent-line      engineering-lead's case; a line is inserted inside ORG-RECORD
  stamp-adopted          no ORG-RECORD, skipped BY NAME - `adopted 1` and a run that
                         saw nothing are different claims
  stamp-drift            MISMATCH, exit 1, not rewritten
  stamp-drift-execute    the same with --execute on, because the refusal is the
                         behaviour and not the display mode; `after` proves the stale
                         digest is still on disk

Proven on a real tree before committing: recomputed one digest independently and it
matched, a second run reported ok 6 / stamped 0, a one-line edit to ## Procedure
produced MISMATCH and reverting it produced ok 6 again.
