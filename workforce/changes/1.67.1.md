finished report and blocked it. A removal or negation marker in the 40 characters
before the match now clears it, and every match in a sentence is checked so a real
park later in the same sentence still blocks. Re-measured: 45 fires in 3,553 turns
(1.27%), all hand-read, none wrong. Three new fixtures, each proven by breaking.
