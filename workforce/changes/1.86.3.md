Relayed and quantified claims seat a juror, and replay-seating can pin its baseline

Two seat-only claim trips, never a gate: `quantified` (none of the X, the
only X we..., no X ... has/shows/meets, not one) seats taste in any domain,
and `relayed` seats sight when a reply sentence shares a four-word run
with the turn's hand-back testimony and carries a count or quantifier the
testimony also carries. Both are tried every time. Measured over 1,330
mutze Stops: +4.5 seats per 100 Stops; 10 of 15 sampled quantified hits
were real set claims. On mutze 30e77777, Stops 284, 635 and 726 now seat;
614 does not.

bin/replay-seating --before <rev|release> runs every before-side script
at that revision, so a committed change still shows its delta.
